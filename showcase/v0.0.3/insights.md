# 《BIS Annual Economic Report 2026 (完整版)》核心观点报告

> 覆盖范围：第 0–82 节（含 10 个专题框）。数据截止 2026 年 5 月 29 日，付印 2026 年 6 月 22 日。Endnotes / Additional notes to graphs / References 等章后内容不计入。

## 总览

**1. 此前的经济韧性建立在三个特定条件之上，而三者正同时承压。**
> "The resilience that characterised the global economy through the review period rested on a specific set of conditions – accommodative financial conditions, synchronised monetary easing and an investment boom sustained by expected transformative technological progress from AI. Each is coming under strain."
解读：报告拒绝"经济具有结构性韧性"的主流叙事，把韧性重新定义为依赖宽松金融条件、同步宽松、AI 投资繁荣的暂时状态。三条支柱同向走弱，意味着增长与金融条件可能同步恶化——这是本年度最上层的框架判断。

**2. 财政空间不再由长期基本面决定，而由市场流动性状态决定。**
> "Government bond market liquidity may seem ample for extended periods but can vanish abruptly, driving up borrowing costs. As a result, fiscal space can shrink well before public debt reaches limits suggested by long-run fundamentals."
解读：财政约束的触发点从"偿付能力/债务阈值"改判为"市场功能与中介能力"。这意味着主权压力事件可以远早于基本面预警发生，风险监测指标必须从债务率转向市场流动性与中介资产负债表健康度（第二章 Box B 给出完整机制）。

**3. 出现"新型财政-金融稳定关联"，央行干预将趋于常态化。**
> "This new fiscal-financial stability nexus amplifies and accelerates the transmission of market stress. … Central banks may need to intervene more frequently to address market dysfunction."
解读：即便银行偿付力无恙，NBFI（尤其高杠杆对冲基金）的融资依赖本身即可放大主权冲击。央行作为市场功能维护者的角色从"应急例外"转为"更频繁的常态"，对央行资产负债表中枢、准备金需求与独立性边界构成长期含义。

**4. 供给冲击已常态化，能源冲击的通胀效应是非线性的。**
> "Adverse supply shocks have become more frequent and more intense (Graph 17.A) – a condition that is set to stay."
> "the inflationary impact may scale non-linearly with the size and duration of the oil disruption."
解读：本轮霍尔木兹冲击的供给缺口（13%）超过 1970 年代（约 8%），且冲击越大单位通胀弹性越高、上游成本时滞会在油价回落后继续推升通胀。政策设计原则因此从"最优化"转向"稳健性"，"忽略暂时冲击"的教科书立场被附加熔断条件。

**5. 稳定币整体宏观影响温和，但其结构性影响集中在货币主权、银行融资与财政空间三处。**
> "the projected effect is quantitatively modest" … "greater household demand for stablecoins could make banks' funding more expensive and less stable … greater demand for sovereign bonds from stablecoin issuers could reduce government interest expenses and create fiscal space."
解读：以美国数据校准的模型显示，即使稳定币市值达 1–3 万亿美元，中期净产出效应为温和负值（银行融资渠道压过财政空间渠道）。真正重要的不是总量，而是分配与制度后果：EMDE 的"稳定币美元化"、银行 LCR/NSFR 与中小企业信贷、以及主权短端融资成本的重塑。

## 前言（第 0–6 节）

**观点 1：能源冲击已扩散到农业与制造业投入品，核心悬念是通胀是否重演 2021–23。**
> "Global headline inflation picked up shortly after the conflict in the Middle East began, and prices of plastics and fertilisers – key inputs – have risen by 30% and 50%, respectively. The central question is whether these initial price increases will broaden and persist, as during 2021–23."
解读：报告用塑料 +30%、化肥 +50% 量化供应链二轮传导路径，而非停留在油价。化肥直接牵动食品价格与低收入国家财政补贴压力。
新颖性：高——多数叙事只盯油价与整体 CPI。重要性：高——化肥是食品通胀的先行变量，对新兴市场构成非对称风险。

**观点 2：通胀预期脱锚速度可能快于历史常态。**
> "Given that it will take several quarters to purge the imbalances in oil physical markets, further volatility in energy prices could arise. In turn, inflation expectations could de-anchor more quickly than in the past."
解读：后疫情通胀记忆使预期管理的容错空间收窄，即使实际二轮效应有限，预期本身也更脆弱。
新颖性：高——对"锚定良好"的宽松假设直接构成挑战。重要性：高——直接影响利率路径与久期风险定价。

**观点 3：AI 资本开支存在周期性反转风险。**
> "The current surge in capital expenditure could prove unsustainable if supply bottlenecks restrain production. Intense competition for market leadership may fuel overinvestment further, as seen in previous innovation waves, increasing the risk of a sharp reversal if AI payoffs disappoint."
解读：把 AI 投资定性为潜在"泡沫式"周期，并点出融资不透明、核心市场高杠杆、私人信贷扩张三类放大器。
新颖性：高——在 AI 乐观情绪主导市场时作出逆共识警示。重要性：高——AI 投资是 2025 年增长支柱，"支柱折断"即系统性风险。

**观点 4（跨章节核心）：财政风险重定价对通胀的方向是模糊的。**
> "While weaker demand is disinflationary, a reassessment of fiscal risk may become inflationary if it triggers exchange rate depreciation or disrupts inflation expectations."
解读：打破"财政恶化自动等于通缩"的直觉——若引发汇率贬值或预期受扰，财政风险转为通胀性，且"货币政策可信度弱"时该渠道更容易被激活。
新颖性：高——罕见的双向开放表述。重要性：高——决定主权压力下应配置通缩资产还是通胀对冲，且取决于可监测的央行信誉变量。

**观点 5：政策上拒绝为财政让路。**
> "In this environment, prioritising medium-term price stability remains key, even where policy actions may have near-term adverse fiscal implications."
解读：在"高债务下财政主导"压力下明确表态，同时认可向 NBFI 扩容量化流动性工具（须"temporary, targeted and reversible"）。
新颖性：中——BIS 一贯立场，但在高债务语境下重申具有政策边界含义。重要性：高——直接关系央行独立性边界与财政主导风险定价。

**观点 6：BIS 的官方创新路径是"央行准备金锚定的代币化平台"。**
> "Project Agorá, a collaboration among eight central banks, the BIS and over 40 private sector institutions, demonstrates how integrating commercial bank deposits and central bank reserves on a programmable platform can enhance cross-border payments."
解读：以 Agorá 为旗舰案例，表明 BIS 主张的路径是升级两级体系，而非公链稳定币。
新颖性：中——项目已公开，但被定位为"官方优先路径"具政策取向意义。重要性：中——影响跨境支付基础设施的竞争格局。

## 第一章 I. Progress and peril

### 1.1 韧性开局与关税冲击被大幅高估

**观点 1：有效关税率远低于宣布水平，全球产出损失下修约三分之一。**
> "the effective average US tariff rate stabilised at 10% in the second half of 2025, significantly lower than the peak announcement of more than 25% (Graph 2.A). Accounting for this lowers the global output loss by about a third from initial estimates."
解读：用"有效税率 vs 宣布税率"的落差量化关税虚张声势，是对全球增长与通胀预期的重要校准。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

![Graph 2.A 有效关税率实际稳定在 10%，远低于宣布的 25%+ 峰值](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_019.png)

**观点 2：关税成本分摊为"企业吸收三分之二、消费者承担三分之一"。**
> "Estimates for US firms suggest those most affected by the tariffs absorbed about two thirds of the cost increases through lower profits, while only passing on one third to consumers (Graph 2.C)."
解读：解释了为何关税未立即推高通胀——代价是美国企业利润率受损；若利润缓冲耗尽，通胀传导可能后置式上升。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 3：中国出口结构向价值链上游迁移。**
> "Moreover, China's increased competitiveness in higher value added goods, whose exports grew by 13% in 2025, also helped propel its overall exports."
解读：对美出口下滑被"对亚洲其他地区出口增加（转口）"与高附加值商品出口 +13% 抵消。
新颖性：中。重要性：中。

### 1.2 通胀回归：非线性的能源冲击

**观点 4：通胀对能源冲击的响应是非线性的，且上游成本时滞会延长压力。**
> "the inflationary impact may scale non-linearly with the size and duration of the oil disruption. Evidence for AEs suggests that inflation is more sensitive to larger energy price shocks (Graph 8.C). … Given the time lags in production, the upstream cost increases could continue to pressure inflation well after energy flows and oil prices normalise."
解读：否定了"通胀是一次性价格水平上移、可被忽略"的假设——冲击越大弹性越高，且压力会在油价正常化后继续释放。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——若成立，降息路径与久期定价需大幅重估。

![Graph 8.A 冲突爆发后全球通胀跳升，化肥与塑料等上游投入品涨幅达两位数](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_028.png)

**观点 5：实物原油价格反应远超期货基准，部分区域油价回到 2008 年中水平。**
> "Brent prices surged by 67% to an intraday peak of $120 in less than two weeks … Physical crude prices have reacted even more to tighter supply conditions, with some regional crude oil prices matching mid-2008 peaks."
解读：紧张集中在实物市场而非金融价格，意味着基准油价低估了真实紧张度，亚洲炼厂与区域通胀承压。

**观点 6：BIS 罕见承认通胀预期锚定可能已被削弱。**
> "Post-pandemic high inflation may have also weakened the anchoring of inflation expectations."
解读：属自我怀疑式表述，直接削弱"可安全忽略冲击"的前提。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 7（对冲性限定）：能源强度下降、"更有针对性"的纾困、预期弱于 2022 年。**
> "the reduction in energy intensity, which has fallen by more than half in some countries over the past two decades." … "relief spending that is more targeted this time should help strengthen fiscal discipline and lessen the inflationary impact of the current energy shock."
解读：报告在通胀警报中埋入三条反向证据，构成"条件性乐观"，为通胀尾部风险设定上限。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

### 1.3 AI 投资繁荣与"竞赛型过度投资"

**观点 8：AI 资本开支超出自身盈利与自由现金流，行业净经济盈余可能转负。**
> "The five largest hyperscalers are set to spend over a trillion US dollars on AI-related capital expenditure from 2025 through 2026. These commitments are outpacing earnings and the free cash flow of these firms, leading some to issue debt to raise additional financing (Graph 11.A)." … "the net economic surplus – the total payoff less investment costs – declines for the sector as a whole and could turn negative in adverse scenarios."
解读：风险不是"AI 是否有效"，而是"AI 有效但回报被过度投资吃掉"。历史类比（运河狂热、英国铁路狂热、1920s 电气化、dotcom）共同特征是"真实技术突破吸引超过商业回报可支撑的资本"。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——若净盈余转负，触发的是融资骤停→投资萧条→金融条件收紧的链式反应。

**观点 9：供给瓶颈反而放大过度投资。**
> "Fast-growing demand for computing power is already pressuring electricity prices and input costs, with potential spillovers to inflation." … "these temporary shortages may also amplify over-investment, as firms attempt to lock in future capacity through long-dated contracts that further expose them to any disappointments in demand."
解读：电力、先进半导体、电网设备三重瓶颈并不冷却投资，而是通过长约把周期风险变成结构性承诺风险；AI 从"通缩性技术进步"变成"阶段性通胀源"。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### Box A: Global supply chains: adaptability and vulnerability

**观点 10（完整论点链条）：脱钩实为"改道"，供应链由区域重构维持韧性。**
> "China's foreign direct investment in ASEAN nations has more than tripled, rising from $10 billion in 2017 to over $34 billion in 2024. Consequently, as the share of US imports from China has steadily declined, US imports from other Asian economies have grown proportionally (Graph A1.A)."
解读：关税 → 中国企业赴东盟设厂 → 美国自华进口份额下降但自其他亚洲经济体进口上升 → 亚洲区域内美国进口市场份额稳定在约 60%。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——直接影响对关税有效性与东盟前景的判断。

**观点 11：AI 投资约贡献美国 2025 年实际 GDP 增长的 1 个百分点。**
> "In the United States, for example, AI-related investments contributed roughly one percentage point to the country's real GDP growth in 2025, by some estimates."
解读：把 2025 年增长韧性归因于单一引擎，凸显对 AI 支出周期的高度依赖。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 12："低价值但不可替代"的关键投入品（氦气）构成隐藏单点故障。**
> "Although helium constitutes a small portion of the semiconductor manufacturing value chain, it is indispensable due to its unique properties as a chemically inert and highly conductive cleaning and cooling agent."
解读：霍尔木兹关闭冲击卡塔尔氦气供应，威胁亚洲半导体制造——脆弱性来自"关键性≠高价值"。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### Box B: The persistent economic effects of the Iran conflict

**观点 13（论点链核心）：海峡长期关闭迫使海湾产油国"关井"，减产超区域供给一半。**
> "The prolonged closure of the strait has forced the 'shut-in' of existing wells, as Gulf producers ran out of storage space. Shut-ins have curtailed about 12 million barrels per day from a total regional crude oil supply of 21 million barrels per day (Graph B1.C)."
解读：关井不仅是暂时停产，还可能造成油井结构性损伤、部分成熟油井永久失能。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——支撑"供给损失具长期甚至永久性"的判断。

**观点 14：大国同步消耗战略储备后的补库需求，将把实物市场紧张延长至 2027。**
> "As strategic reserves are being drawn down simultaneously across large consumers (United States, China, Japan and the European Union), the rebuilding of those reserves could keep the physical markets tight for several months, possibly well into 2027 (Graph B1.B)."
解读：补库从"抑制油价的力量"变成"延长紧张期的力量"。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——能源高企可能持续到 2027，深刻影响通胀路径与利率预期。

**观点 15：供给缺口的量级超越历史能源危机，而缓冲工具严重不足。**
> "a cut in crude oil flow of over 10 million barrels a day, equivalent to 13% of normal supply (Graph 4.B). By comparison, supply losses in the 1970s energy crises were around 8%." … "The strategic reserve release of 400 million barrels by the International Energy Agency (IEA), the largest in history, covered only 20 days of lost Hormuz flow."
解读：史上最大释储仅覆盖 20 天缺口；九国逾 40 处能源资产受损，卡塔尔 LNG 产能削减 17%、恢复需长达五年。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

![Graph B1.A 霍尔木兹冲击造成的重大物流与生产中断及其外溢](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_024.png)

**观点 16：市场定价与实体风险显著脱节。**
> "Market-implied volatility and tail risk indicators have moved back close to pre-conflict levels. This benign view contrasts with lingering uncertainty regarding the persistent ramifications of damage already done, particularly for inflation."
解读：波动率已回到冲突前水平，与"通胀遗留不确定性"形成背离——典型的逆向风险提示。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### Box C: Transformative AI, long-term growth and r-star

**观点 17（论点链第一环）：AI 作用于"知识生产"这一增长的最紧约束。**
> "AI could go further by augmenting the production of knowledge itself. If, at some point, AI systems can improve their own capabilities and 'create' technology and ideas, the macroeconomic consequences could be profoundly different from past innovations. A key constraint on long-run growth, namely the rate at which humans can generate new ideas, could be lifted." … "Trammell and Korinek (2023) also argue that economies currently operate in this complementarity regime, implying that the transformative scenario remains a possibility rather than a present reality."
解读：第二环是 Jones-Tonetti 的"最弱环节"互补性——若关键任务仍由人类承担且强互补，总产出被最慢环节卡住。结论：当前仍处于互补性区间，"变革性 AI"是可能性而非现实。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——是评估长期中性利率与潜在增长路径的框架基础。

**观点 18（论点链第二环，第四情景）："需求瓶颈"使生产率因需求缺失而非技术限制而停滞。**
> "The consumer base could erode as productive capacity expands. Forward-looking firms, recognising the shrinking future market for AI-produced goods and services, may find it unprofitable to invest in innovating and automating the next task. Productivity stalls not because of technological limitation, but because the demand to justify further capacity expansion is missing."
解读：自动化把收入从劳动（高消费倾向）转移到资本（低消费倾向）并回流为 AI 投资，消费基础萎缩；前瞻性企业停止自动化投资。这是对"AI 必然带来生产率跃升"的最强反驳。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——若该情景主导，长期是通缩+低利率，与"通胀+高利率"的结论方向相反。

**观点 19（政策落点）：AI 对 r-star 与通胀的**方向**是情景依赖的。**
> "Under the demand bottleneck scenario, r-star initially rises while supply side forces dominate but then falls below its pre-AI baseline as the bottleneck takes hold." … "The r-star implications thus depend on parameters that are uncertain today: how much AI profits and innovation rely on consumer demand, how quickly competition within the AI sector erodes margins, and how fast AI products and infrastructure become obsolete."
解读：生产率主导→r-star 与通胀上行；需求瓶颈主导→r-star 先升后跌破 AI 前基线、通胀转通缩。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——直接否定"AI 必然推高利率"的单一押注，并给出三个可观测判别参数。

### 1.4 金融脆弱性作为放大器

**观点 20：市场对增长的隐含要求高于企业自身历史最好成绩。**
> "The implied long-term earnings growth for the largest corporations sits well above recent historical benchmarks (Graph 12.A) … These implied rates often exceed even the elevated growth that some of the technology firms have delivered in their relatively short lifetimes." … "Risk premia on the largest US stocks have compressed markedly since the Covid-19 pandemic … This points to growing investor complacency and reduced compensation for risk-bearing."
解读：判据不是估值倍数，而是"价格隐含长期 EPS 增速 > 企业自身历史实现增速"；大盘股风险补偿分布整体左移。
新颖性：中高。重要性：高——估值对增长容错度极低。

![Graph 12.A 股价隐含的长期盈利增长远超企业历史实现水平](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_034.png)

**观点 21：美股集中度与家庭股市敞口使回调成为全球宏观事件。**
> "with US stocks accounting for an outsized share of global equity markets – about 64% of the MSCI Global index – the wealth impact from a US-led repricing could propagate globally." … "Household equity exposures have grown over the past few decades, both relative to total wealth (Graph 12.C) and income."
解读：财富效应→消费收缩的传导比过去更陡，美联储"看跌期权"的有效边界被推前。
新颖性：中高。重要性：高。

**观点 22：AI 生态存在"隐藏杠杆"，且信用市场与股票市场信号背离。**
> "The terms of such deals are typically poorly disclosed, with risks of the same asset being pledged multiple times." … "The credit spreads of some AI firms have already begun to widen somewhat to reflect this risk (Graph 13.B), even as equity markets continue to price in significant upside gains."
解读：循环融资（芯片商/超算商持股 AI 实验室、后者承诺多年采购）占 AI 实验室融资约一半，存在同一资产重复质押风险；信用市场通常先于股市定价风险。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——股债定价背离是典型的脆弱结构。

**观点 23：私人信贷的传染链已出现早期压力证据。**
> "Direct lending funds … have quadrupled their lending to the AI and information technology (IT) sectors in the past five years, to about 15% of their portfolios." … "direct lending funds catering to retail investors have faced mounting redemption requests, forcing some to liquidate assets and return capital despite having no contractual obligation to do so." … "banks' growing and opaque exposure to private credit funds, compounded by overlapping ties through insurance companies' balance sheets."
解读：传导链为零售渠道赎回 → 私人信贷基金被迫返资 → 银行与保险的不透明敞口 → 创造大量就业的中小企业。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### 1.5 财政承压与政策含义（第 19–23 节）

**观点 24：r−g 转正，靠名义增长自动稀释债务的时代结束。**
> "The gap between bond yields and nominal GDP growth, previously deeply negative, has reversed and turned positive in many countries (Graph 16.C). Countries can no longer count on nominal growth to stabilise debt dynamics."
解读：债务率稳定现在需要主动的初级盈余，从"被动可持续"切换到"主动紧缩"。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——长端收益率结构性上移的论据。

**观点 25：新兴市场财政恶化幅度反而大于发达经济体。**
> "Cyclically adjusted primary deficits in AEs averaged 1.9% of GDP from 2022 onwards … nearly double the 1.1% recorded over the two preceding decades. EMEs have seen an even sharper deterioration (1.8% since 2022 versus 0.1% between 2000 and 2019)."
解读：直接反驳"EM 财政纪律更好"的叙事。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

**观点 26："通胀救财政"的直觉被拆解为净负效应。**
> "Though higher inflation in isolation may relieve fiscal burden, the required responses from monetary policy to rein in inflation may entail higher nominal and real interest rates. The net effects would be further increases in debt servicing costs, more so if inflationary pressures were higher and more persistent (Graph 16.A). Sizeable refinancing needs amplify this impact."
解读：反通胀 → 付息成本上升 → 财政恶化的反馈；美国 2026 年到期国债占存量约 35%（样本最高）。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

**观点 27：财政与货币政策的分离原则"正承受越来越大的压力"。**
> "Yet higher public debt crowds out monetary space, weakening the central banks' ability to contain inflationary pressures without further worsening public finance. The separation between fiscal and monetary policies – long a cornerstone of central bank independence and credibility – is coming under growing strain."
解读：机制是"高债务挤出货币空间"——反通胀的代价自动包含财政恶化。
新颖性：高——BIS 正式承认独立性基石承压，措辞罕见升级。重要性：高。

**观点 28："忽略暂时性冲击"的立场被附加两个熔断条件。**
> "the rationale to look through temporary supply shocks remains compelling – but only up to a point." … "With more protracted supply shocks and more backward-looking expectation formation, the case for looking through weakens, as the inflation costs rise while the ability to prevent large output losses decline (Graph 17.B)." … "the payoff to investing in inflation credibility has increased."
解读：熔断条件为"冲击更持久 + 预期形成更后视"；此时"额外通胀"由约 1pp 升至约 4pp，而"避免的产出损失"由约 3% 降至约 2%。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——为判断央行何时必须转向提供显式门槛。

**观点 29：财政三难中"可持续性是前两者的前提"。**
> "The overarching task confronting fiscal policy is to reconcile three demands that have become increasingly difficult to satisfy jointly: cushioning households and firms from more frequent supply shocks, meeting rising defence and security commitments, and placing public finance on a sustainable path. The first two cannot be delivered credibly if the third is not."
解读：以"可信度约束"而非"资源约束"表述财政边界；普遍补贴与价格上限的批评落点是退出迟滞（"outlast the circumstances"）而非短期成本。
新颖性：中高。重要性：高。

**观点 30：三条"货币逃逸阀"被逐一点名为禁区。**
> "Pressures on central banks to accommodate fiscal needs – whether through lower interest rates, extended balance sheet support or forbearance on sovereign exposures – may offer apparent short-term reliefs, but they undermine the core pillar on which low and stable financing costs rest."
解读：收益短暂、代价巨大且持久，最终成本由家庭与企业以高通胀形式承担。
新颖性：高——"forbearance on sovereign exposures"被明确列入禁区。重要性：高——长期通胀溢价与实物资产定价的核心准则。

**观点 31：审慎政策拒绝"以简化之名行放松之实"，并把 AI 风险纳入监管视野。**
> "A policy response to these interconnections begins with finalising and implementing Basel III consistently across jurisdictions and resisting calls for broad-based deregulation under the umbrella of simplification." … "Supervisory visibility into AI use by market participants needs to improve to capture model-driven correlated exposures, herding behaviour and the potential for algorithmic collusion."
解读：给出可执行工具（SFT 最低折扣率、开放式基金流动性管理、更广泛中央清算），并把"算法合谋""前沿 AI 可大规模识别漏洞"列为新兴风险。
新颖性：高。重要性：中高。

## 第二章 II. High public debt and shifting financial markets: challenges for central banks

### 2.1 接近历史高位的公共债务与变化中的财政空间

**观点 1：r−g 差对主权危机几乎没有预测力，且往往在危机前才抬升。**
> "By contrast, the interest rate-growth differential seems to have limited predictive power for sovereign debt crises. Such crises have often occurred when this differential was low or negative (Graphs 3.B and 3.C). Moreover, this differential tends to rise only shortly before crises, leaving little time for corrective policy actions."
解读：直接质疑政策界与市场依赖的预警逻辑——不能等 r−g 恶化才行动。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 2：表外与或有负债构成主权资产负债表的隐藏杠杆。**
> "Additional risks to public debt could arise from the losses incurred by state-owned enterprises and subnational governments, which are often backed by explicit and implicit guarantees, and by the fiscal burden of natural disasters."
解读：私人部门高债务（未来救助成本）、国企/地方政府隐性担保、气候灾害负担均不在常规债务统计内。
新颖性：中。重要性：中。

**观点 3：若无对冲措施，2031 年后各地公共债务将大幅上升。**
> "Without offsetting measures such as higher government revenues, other public spending cuts or changes in the interest rate-growth differential, these spending pressures imply a substantial increase in public debt across jurisdictions beyond 2031."
解读：老龄化（养老金/医疗）叠加大规模公共投资（基建、国防、可再生能源）需求，财政压力是结构性的。
新颖性：中。重要性：中——久期溢价与长端利率的结构性上行因素。

![Graph 1.A 政府债务预计继续上升（先进经济体与新兴市场经济体）](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_053.png)

### Box A: Insufficient debt consolidation in good times

**观点 4（论点链核心）：财政对债务的"自动纠正"机制到 2024 年已完全消失。**
> "while primary surpluses increased as debt rose in the 1980s and 1990s, this effect vanished by 2024 (Graph A1.A)."
解读：历史上债务上升会触发基本盈余的制度性反应，如今该反应函数结构性断裂。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——高债务不再内生地触发整顿，债务路径缺乏自我稳定机制。

**观点 5：负 r−g 被误当作"免费财政空间"，反而削弱整顿动机。**
> "One possible explanation is that more negative interest rate-growth (r-g) differentials were seen as creating additional fiscal space. Other factors … including greater 'fiscal fatigue' and greater political polarisation."
解读：形成"低利率→高债务"的反向因果；一旦利率中枢正常化，此前被掩盖的整顿需求会集中爆发。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 6：好时光整顿的关键是制度框架与低初始债务，而非财政规则本身。**
> "several jurisdictions – including Chile, Denmark and Sweden – consolidated in good times and adjusted their primary balances symmetrically. This more countercyclical behaviour appears to be associated with stronger institutional frameworks and lower initial debt levels rather than the mere presence of fiscal rules."
新颖性：高。重要性：中。

### Box B: Why financial intermediation matters for fiscal space

**观点 7（论点链核心）：财政空间可在基本面不变的情况下收缩。**
> "Fiscal space can therefore shrink even if fiscal fundamentals and the long-run paths of interest rates and growth have not changed."
解读：金融中介的资产负债表能力、融资条件与风险约束独立地决定财政空间；紧缩条件下成本曲线上移并变陡、债务上限内移。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——风险判断需盯中介健康度而非只看财政消息。

![Graph B1 金融条件决定财政空间：紧缩条件下主权融资成本曲线上移并变陡](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_059.png)

**观点 8：放大机制不再依赖"投资者协调失败"。**
> "In such markets, balance sheet dynamics and risk constraints can amplify shocks to yields even in the absence of a coordination failure."
解读：超越经典多重均衡/挤兑理论——现实中纯粹的中介风险承担能力约束即可放大收益率冲击。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——若根源是风险承担能力，单靠财政框架或央行兜底无法根治。

**观点 9：三大"标准药方"各有一体两面。**
> "longer maturities, lower foreign-currency issuance and central bank backstops can mitigate rollover risks, but they may also increase exposure to other risks, including duration risk, foreign investor balance sheet constraints and moral hazard related to central bank interventions."
解读：风险只是换位置而非消失。
新颖性：中高。重要性：高——组合层面需重新计量。

### 2.2 新型财政-金融稳定关联

**观点 10：即便银行偿付力无恙，放大通道依然存在。**
> "This has given rise to a new fiscal-financial stability nexus, with amplification channels that operate even when bank solvency risks are contained."
解读：以银行稳健度为核心的风险监测（资本、流动性）不足以覆盖当前主权市场风险。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 11：风险质变在于它首次出现在"财政可持续性长期无需置疑"的主要发达经济体。**
> "What is new is the emergence of these links in several major AEs where fiscal sustainability had rarely been in doubt, alongside significant changes in the structure of the financial system."
解读：核心储备资产发行国的财政-金融联动，是对全球无风险利率定价框架的范式冲击。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### 2.3 NBFI 在国债市场角色的上升

**观点 12：主权债持有结构已完成"官方退出、私人接盘"的转移。**
> "Their share of total sovereign debt holdings rose from 44% in 2021 to 53% in 2025 (Graph 4.A). … As quantitative tightening progressed over 2022–25, the share of government bond holdings by domestic central banks fell from 27% to 17% and that of the foreign official sector from 15% to 13%."
解读：NBFI 已成为最大持有者，市场对流动性/保证金冲击的敏感度系统性抬升。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

![Graph 4.A 先进经济体主权债持有者结构：NBFI 份额由 44% 升至 53%](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_060.png)

**观点 13：对冲基金的双边回购大量以"零折扣"成交，几乎无缓冲。**

解读：零折扣等于允许无约束加杠杆，是回购市场挤兑与强制去杠杆的直接触发点。

**观点 14：银行-主权纽带"由直接转为间接"。**
> "there is evidence that the direct bank-sovereign nexus may have become less pronounced than a decade ago. … At the same time, banks' interconnections with NBFIs seem to have created indirect transmission channels from sovereign risk to banks."
解读：监管关注点应从银行主权债敞口转向银行对 NBFI 的敞口（回购、主经纪、衍生品）。

### 2.4 市场功能、财政政策互动与跨境溢出

**观点 15：高债务使市场功能失调的尾部概率提升约 10 倍。**
> "Estimates indicate that the probability of experiencing a stress event similar to the GFC within the next three months is about 10 times higher when the ratio of public debt to GDP is high than when it is low (approximately 3.8% versus 0.3%)."
解读：高债务本身即是市场功能失调的放大器；NBFI 占比高亦有类似效应。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 16：财政冲击与市场流动性构成双向反馈。**
> "the absolute magnitude of fiscal surprises is larger in illiquid markets (Graph 7.A), and adverse fiscal surprises, in turn, erode market liquidity (Graph 7.B)."
解读：图表注进一步给出量级——一个标准差财政风险冲击抬高 10 年期收益率约 30bp，并把流动性"噪声"推升至其均值约 20 倍。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

**观点 17：约 130 万亿美元的 FX 衍生品把汇率风险转化为展期风险。**

解读：FX 掉期与回购由同一批交易商做市、共享风险预算，"表外但共享风险预算"是跨市场传染的关键。

**观点 18：市场功能失调独立贡献约一半的价格跌幅。**

解读：2022 年英国金边债事件中，财政新闻本身只解释一半波动，其余来自中介机制。

### 2.5 传导受损、央行兜底设计与政策含义（第 38–46 节）

**观点 19：央行被动宽松不能缓冲财政风险重定价，反而放大其后果。**

解读：对高债务下的货币政策选择构成直接约束——被动宽松不是免费保险。

**观点 20：财政扩张的通胀效应取决于"未来整顿预期"。**

解读：财政沟通与中期框架的可信度本身即是通胀风险定价的一部分。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 21：行为学渠道——告知家庭"高债务"即抬升其通胀预期。**
> "surveys across several countries show that informing households about high public debt raises their inflation expectations, with a considerably larger rise among those who have less confidence in the central bank's determination to fight inflation."
解读：财政新闻本身即能推动预期失锚，对央行沟通提出新要求。

This is particularly challenging during stress, as dysfunction often stems from news about fundamentals, such as fiscal shocks."
解读：Bagehot 原则在"财政-金融稳定新关联"下几乎无法干净执行。英国 2022 LDI 干预被固化为模板（窄口径、限时、明确资格与退出，并采用开放式购买而非定额目标）。

Financial conditions could also tighten regardless, through higher term premia and currency depreciation."
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 29：独立性不能替代财政可持续性；整顿的构成与节奏同等重要。**

解读：明确存在"债务陷阱"（高债务抑制增长、弱增长使债务更难削减），破解之道是支出重配到基建、教育与科技创新。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

> "regulatory frameworks that apply similar stringency to financial intermediaries posing similar risks … regardless of their legal form or business model. … implementation is further challenged by a fragmented institutional architecture." … "mandatory central clearing brings its own financial stability risks. 解读：同时指出"最低折扣"的隐藏方向性副作用——在许多回购交易中折扣本是保护抵押品提供方的，统一最低折扣可能单向有利于资金/抵押品出借方。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 31（章末）：拖延会把政策推入"价格稳定/金融稳定/财政可持续"的三选一。**
> "Acting early to strengthen fiscal frameworks, financial resilience and monetary credibility would preserve room for manoeuvre across policy domains. Delay would instead make adjustment more costly and increase the likelihood that future shocks force difficult choices between price stability, financial stability and fiscal sustainability."
新颖性：中。重要性：中高。

**观点 32（关键脚注量化）：稳定美国债务需至少 GDP 10% 的永久性财政调整。**
> "Auclert et al (2025) estimate that US public debt could rise to about 250% of GDP. Importantly, stabilising debt at such high levels would require an implausibly large permanent fiscal adjustment of at least 10% of GDP."（Endnote 7）
解读：远超一般财政辩论的量级，是高债务路径算术上缺乏可信收敛机制的注脚。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

## 第三章 III. Anchoring trust in money: innovation beyond stablecoins

### Box A: The historical emergence of money and of central banking

**观点 1（论点链·背景）：货币先于技术，起源于信用关系。**
> "the earliest forms of money were simple credit relationships … Over time, as societies and their web of credit commitments became larger, promises to pay were replaced by commodity money, such as cowry shells or coins made of natural alloys like electrum."
解读：商品货币在旧世界与新世界独立出现，凸显货币的根本重要性。
新颖性：中。重要性：中——为"货币是制度成就而非技术产物"提供长时段证据。

**观点 2（论点链·机制与警示）：阿姆斯特丹银行的兴衰说明"支撑机构"决定信任。**
> "the Bank of Amsterdam (Amsterdamsche Wisselbank), founded in 1609 with the stated purpose to 'check the confusion of coin' … This was apparent, for instance, in the crisis of 1763 … the Bank stepped in to stabilise the system … Yet its downfall in the 1780s, precipitated by large-scale lending to an affiliated entity and a lack of fiscal backing, shows the limits of fiat money and how trust can falter when the institutions backing it are insufficiently robust."
解读：既是最后贷款人雏形（1763），又因关联放贷与财政支撑缺失而崩塌（1780s）——历史直接类比当代私人货币。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 3（论点链·结论）：结算与清算业务是"自然垄断"。**
> "in 1805 the Battle of Trafalgar cut continental Europe off from the flow of Spanish silver dollars … This shock precipitated a major contraction of lending and severe economic fallout in France and Spain." … "The Bank assessed the quality of private banknotes and would even stamp the notes of failed banks with red ink … but the large profits of the institution underscored that this business is a natural monopoly."
解读：美国自由银行时期萨福克银行（1825–58）的成功及其高利润，论证公共机构接管（1913 年美联储）的必然性。
新颖性：中。重要性：中。

### 3.1 货币信任的基础、根基属性与网络效应（第 50–56 节）

**观点 4（全章统一原则）：技术必须服务于货币的公共品功能。**
> "The unifying principle is that technology should serve – not undermine – the core public good functions of money." / "History has shown that money is far more than a technology; it is an institutional achievement."
解读：为全章对稳定币与代币化的评判设定规范性标尺。
新颖性：中。重要性：中。

**观点 5：货币的本质是"无需置疑即被接受为最终结算"。**
> "What fundamentally matters, however, is that money is accepted 'with no questions asked' as final settlement of an obligation. This status is what makes prices meaningful, contracts enforceable and debts dischargeable."
解读：用"信息不敏感"取代教科书三大职能作为本质标准。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中。

**观点 6：单一定价（singleness）的微小缺失在压力下即崩塌。**
> "The absence of par exchange, even if tiny, may suffice in fair weather, but it disintegrates when stress hits, undermining the very property that enables coordination."
解读：平静期的可接受性不构成安全，直接针对稳定币二级市场脱离面值问题。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 7：流动性供给的"弹性"不可替代。**
> "If the supply of those balances were rigid, payments would jam and an otherwise soluble liquidity shock could mutate into a solvency crisis. Central banks avert such outcomes by supplying intraday credit for settlement and by standing ready with backstops in times of stress."
新颖性：中。重要性：中高——隐含对固定供给型货币的否定。

**观点 8：网络效应可逆，协调可能突然崩溃。**
> "if singleness frays or elasticity is insufficient, network externalities work in reverse and coordination can collapse suddenly." … "fragmented systems trap liquidity, force costly intermediation across networks and erode the perception of singleness."
解读：把央行定位为"系统的受托人（trustee）"——所有其他资产负债表可安全围绕其运转。
新颖性：中高。重要性：中高。

**观点 9：两级架构不可替代，其效率来自"信任社会化"。**
> "The central bank alone cannot deliver the no-questions-asked property of money. Commercial banks and other (non-bank) regulated intermediaries are indispensable." … "The system's architecture socialises trust, leaving individual transactions free from the burden of bilateral due diligence."
新颖性：中。重要性：中——为"去中介"叙事设反论。

**观点 10：代币化创新被定性为"演进而非新的货币形态"。**
> "These represent an evolution rather than introducing genuinely new forms of money. Their prospects turn less on novelty than on whether they connect to the existing unit of account, preserve singleness through credible redeemability in central bank money at par with finality … If not, they risk reviving the very frictions that the modern system evolved to solve."
解读：成败取决于能否接入既有记账单位与平价可兑性，而非技术创新本身。
新颖性：高。重要性：中高。

### Box B: The economics of public permissionless blockchains

**观点 11（论点链·机制）：公链的现实是"租金、负外部性与拥堵"，且属结构性特征。**
> "the current reality of public permissionless blockchain ecosystems is one of rents, negative externalities and congestion, with fragmentation eroding network benefits. … These challenges are not merely technical flaws, but structural features of decentralised systems."
解读：代币经济学以费用/共识机制（PoW、PoS、委托验证者）激励诚实行为，但交易量上升即推高手续费与确认时间；扭曲公平、抬升成本、削弱用户留存，侵蚀网络长期可持续性与扩展性。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——对"公链将成为下一代支付轨道"构成根本性质疑。

**观点 12（论点链·结论）：为提效而采用的方案实质是"去中心化的隐性回退"。**

The solutions proposed to address these challenges may improve efficiency and reduce costs for individual networks, yet they reflect a shift away from the vision of decentralisation and the removal of intermediaries."
新颖性：高。重要性：中。

### 3.2 稳定币的兴起、规模与当前安排的问题（第 57–65 节）

**观点 13：法币锚定稳定币几乎完全美元化（99.4%），储备已达"大型主权/政府 MMF"量级。**
> "To date, 99.4% of fiat-backed stablecoins (by market valuation) are pegged to the US dollar – thereby leveraging on the unit of account provided by the leading international reserve currency. … Their holdings of Treasury bills have risen to levels comparable with those of large jurisdictions and government MMFs."
解读：稳定币储备已成为美国短端国债市场不可忽视的边际买家，实质借用美元的记账单位功能。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——对短端利率、货币市场流动性与美国融资成本有实质影响。

![Graph 2.A 稳定币市值约 3200 亿美元，高度集中于 USDT/USDC 且近期趋于停滞](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_098.png)

**观点 14：交易量被严重夸大，且"监管即能催生非美元稳定币"被证伪。**
> "Annual stablecoin transaction volume amounted to an estimated $28 trillion in 2025, equivalent to less than three business weeks of settlement volumes of the largest US wholesale payment systems; values net of transactions between wallets owned by the same party are far lower. … robust domestic regulatory frameworks have not, by themselves, catalysed large non-US dollar regulation-compliant stablecoin markets."
解读：28 万亿美元仅相当于美国最大批发支付系统不到三周的结算量；清晰监管并未点活非美元合规稳定币市场。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——同时纠偏"稳定币已具系统重要性"与"监管能塑造多极货币格局"两项假设。

**观点 15：分析框架创新——区分"设计问题"与"轨道问题"。**
> "First are issues related to the design of stablecoin arrangements, including financial integrity and moneyness … Second are issues related to the rails – the operation, scalability, security, governance and settlement properties of public permissionless ledgers. This distinction clarifies what ultimately depends on credible institutions, sound regulation and legal certainty, and what needs to be addressed by network design."
新颖性：中。重要性：中——把政策讨论从"好事/坏事"转向"哪类问题归谁解决"。

**观点 16：多职能加密中介制造"准存款"，却无缓冲与并表监管。**
> "much stablecoin activity is handled by multifunctional cryptoasset intermediaries whose borrowing activities create short-term redeemable liabilities economically similar to deposits, but typically without capital/liquidity buffers or consolidated supervision."
解读：若出现挤兑，将是"类银行存款"挤兑但无存款保险与央行后盾。
新颖性：中。重要性：高。

**观点 17：链上冻结不能替代常态化 AML/CFT；"设计即诚信"倾向许可制。**
> "such measures cannot replace routine, large-scale AML/CFT controls in everyday payments." … "Permissioned platforms that embed AML/CFT pre-screening, sanctions screening and auditable data trails in the transaction flow – while aligning with data sovereignty constraints – could uphold financial integrity at scale."
解读：无许可公链的跨境性质带来持久监管套利风险；BIS 明确偏好许可制轨道。
新颖性：高。重要性：中。

**观点 18：当前稳定币"更像 ETF 份额而非支付手段"。**
> "stablecoin transfers settle neither directly nor indirectly on central bank balance sheets. By construction, they cannot currently ensure exchange at par across issuers and blockchains under all conditions. … Redemption frictions are common, indicating that current stablecoin designs resemble exchange-traded fund (ETF) shares rather than a means of payment."
解读：若在法律/经济属性上被归为证券或 ETF，监管归属、投资者保护与资本要求都将不同。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 19：供给弹性受"现金预付"发行模式约束，无法内生创造信用。**
> "Elasticity, in turn, remains constrained by a cash-in-advance issuance model and by the liquidity and market depth of reserve assets. Easing these balance sheet constraints would require stablecoins to achieve broad payments acceptance, enabling issuers to acquire assets and extend credit through new coin issuance rather than by drawing on their bank balances."
新颖性：中。重要性：中——稳定币的货币乘数效应远逊于银行，货币总量分析不应等同银行存款。

**观点 20：货币型稳定币的前置条件是央行货币级储备与常备便利。**
> "Money-like stablecoins, particularly if used at scale, would require par redeemability at all times, anchored in low-risk reserves and credible backstops. … Absent redemption frictions, some form of standing facility access or equivalent support would be necessary to contain runs and prevent destabilising fire sales of reserves."
解读：等于说没有央行背书就不可能是货币；投资型设计（放松平价、类 ETF）则更适合储值（含 EMDE 的 FX 敞口需求）而非支付。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——为"货币型 vs 投资型"画出政策分界线。

### Box C: Regulatory frameworks for stablecoins

**观点 21（论点链·共性）：六辖区监管在三大支柱上一致。**
> "a comparison of jurisdictions where regulatory frameworks have been developed or are already in effect – the European Union (EU), Hong Kong SAR, Japan, Singapore, the United Kingdom and the United States – highlights common pillars of par redemption, fully backed reserves and restrictions on interest to holders. Approaches diverge in redemption conditions, reserve composition, central bank access and the calibration of capital."
解读：**平价赎回、全额储备、禁止向持有人付息**是共识；分歧集中在赎回条件、储备构成、**央行准入**与资本校准。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——监管套利空间集中体现在央行准入与资本规则。

**观点 22（论点链·具体校准）：英国 40% 无息央行存款要求；美国 GENIUS 严格限制再抵押。**
> "the Bank of England proposes that systemic sterling stablecoins maintain at least 40% of backing as unremunerated central bank deposits and up to 60% in short-dated UK government securities … The US GENIUS Act prescribes identifiable one-for-one reserves consisting of US currency, Federal Reserve balances, insured bank deposits, short-dated US Treasuries, certain Treasury-backed repos and qualifying government money market instruments, with strict limits on rehypothecation."
解读：储备须留存境内 + 限制再抵押，直接影响稳定币储备对境外 T-bill/货币市场的需求与流动性传导。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### 3.3 稳定币的宏观金融影响：三情景框架（第 67–70 节）

**观点 23：三个储备情景（批发存款/短期国债/央行储备）对应不同的第一轮资产负债表冲击。**
> "In a first scenario, issuers hold only wholesale deposits at banks; in a second, they hold short-dated government bonds (eg treasury bills); and in a third, issuers hold central bank reserves."
解读：以家庭用存款购买 100 美元稳定币为例——国债情景下发行方向 NBFI 买 75、向银行买 25；央行储备情景下银行准备金直接减少 100（对银行冲击最大），且该情景对无银行牌照的发行方"在很多辖区并不可能实现"。
新颖性：中。重要性：中——储备资产选择是宏观影响差异的根源。

**观点 24：三情景共同结果是银行融资地位被削弱。**
> "All three scenarios feature an initial weakening of the banking sector's funding position, potentially triggering adjustments by banks that could affect credit provision. Clearly, the effects will depend strongly on the distribution of the impact across banks and available alternatives to bank credit."
新颖性：中。重要性：中。

![Graph 4 稳定币储备资产选择的第一轮资产负债表效应（三情景映射）](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_108.png)

**观点 25（被低估的传导渠道）：流动性指标恶化 + 中小企业信贷的分配效应。**
> "Banks' asset levels are initially unchanged at the sector level, but liquidity metrics deteriorate on average. This is because regulatory run-off rates for wholesale deposits are higher than for retail deposits (reducing the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)) and wholesale deposits are considered a less stable source of medium-term funding (reducing the Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR)). … Credit to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), if primarily supplied by smaller banks, could thus be adversely affected."
解读：零售转批发存款存在分配效应——大银行更受益，SME 信贷若主要依赖小银行将受不利影响。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——一条被低估的实体传导渠道。

**观点 26：三种金融稳定风险的分类学。**
> "In the government bill reserve scenario, sizeable holdings of bills by stablecoin issuers would raise the possibility of asymmetric, outsize effects on sovereign yield curves during episodes of large redemptions." … "In the central bank reserve scenario, users might consider the stablecoin a close substitute for central bank money. This would raise the risk of large, rapid reallocations of liquidity between banks and stablecoin issuers in times of stress."
解读：国债储备→赎回时对主权曲线超比例冲击并传导至货币市场；存款储备→逃向稳定币的"安全转移"；央行储备→稳定币被视为央行货币的近似替代品。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### Box D: Quantifying the impact of stablecoins on bank lending and fiscal space

**观点 27（论点链·两个反向渠道）：**
> "Two countervailing channels that determine the macroeconomic impact are at work in this framework: a bank lending channel and a fiscal space channel (Graph D1). The bank lending channel arises because stablecoin demand by households raises deposit rates and increases banks' funding costs … The fiscal space channel, in turn, arises due to stablecoin issuers' demand for short-term government bonds. As stablecoin issuers bid up the price of treasury bills, the government's funding costs decline."
解读：银行贷款渠道（融资成本上升→信贷收缩→投资产出下降）与财政空间渠道（压低 T-bill 收益率→政府融资成本下降→减税/扩支→产出上升）相互对冲，净效应方向不定。
新颖性：高。重要性：高——为所有稳定币宏观讨论提供可检验框架。

![Graph D1 稳定币影响的两个渠道：银行贷款 vs 财政空间](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_108.png)

**观点 28（关键非对称）：高债务/高外需可翻转符号；央行储备是否付息决定正负。**
> "The aggregate output response is modestly negative under the assumption that stablecoins hold government bills as reserve assets but high public debt and high foreign demand can change the direction of this effect… In a central bank reserve scenario, the model suggests that the net output effect of widespread stablecoin adoption could turn positive. … the central bank receives the seigniorage revenue … The effect weakens for remunerated reserves."
解读：即便市值达 1/2/3 万亿美元，效应在量级上仍温和；但"央行对稳定币储备是否付息"是一个极强的政策杠杆——付息则该情景退化为国债情景，正效应消失。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

**观点 29：稳定币压低主权短端收益率、创造财政空间，但伴随回购抵押品萎缩。**
> "Empirical evidence suggests that increases in stablecoin market capitalisation can push down the short end of sovereign yield curves in issuing jurisdictions. Persistent growth in stablecoins could therefore lower short-term funding costs of some sovereign issuers, providing additional fiscal space to cut taxes or expand spending … In addition, collateral available for repo transactions could shrink."
解读：成本侧还包括钱市波动加剧、逃税侵蚀税基、铸币税损失（央行准备金/现金需求净下降）。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

### Box E: Deposit dollarisation and stablecoins

**观点 30（论点链·基线）：存款美元化数十年几乎不变。**
> "the prevalence of deposit dollarisation – which shares certain similarities with dollarisation via stablecoins in its current form – has changed little over the past decades (Graph E1.A). … Foreign stablecoins could lead to further dollarisation by providing fast and round-the-clock access to foreign currency assets, potentially bypassing domestic foreign exchange (FX) restrictions and capital controls. 解读：中位数美元化比率 1995–2019 大致稳定在约 20–30%，但货币危机后（t+2 至 t+6）升至约 40–50% 且持续；高通胀+汇率弹性低体制下约 30%，低通胀+浮动约 15%。稳定币是打破这一稳定态的边际力量。
新颖性：高。重要性：高。

解读：评估风险时应区分"货币需求不稳"与"丧失通胀控制"两个不同量级的后果。

### 3.4 稳定币美元化、下一代货币体系与结论（第 74–79 节）

**观点 33：通过稳定币实现的美元化可能相当持久。**

**观点 34：稳定币已形成"加密—外汇市场"接驳渠道。**

解读：同时提示"稳定币储备快速抛售可能触发央行干预"与"外币稳定币高需求经济体将被动承受更紧金融条件"。

**观点 38：不做全面代币化也可以获得可编程性收益。**
> "For wholesale transactions, upgrades to real-time gross settlement (RTGS) systems can deliver tangible benefits, including basic forms of programmability, without necessarily moving towards a fully tokenised financial system." … "Broader non-bank access to central bank settlement accounts, or liquidity facilities and backstops, can bring benefits in some cases, but it also raises questions on risk management, supervision and necessary guardrails."
解读：为"非代币化的渐进改良"正名，降低对全代币化架构的强制依赖；统一账本以央行货币为锚，承载代币化存款、稳定币与代币化资产。

![Graph 9 统一账本架构：以央行货币为锚，整合代币化存款、稳定币与代币化资产](/agent-images/source:6rxddpszvsnganzpbxv8/page_117.png)

**观点 39（结论·准入底线）：**
> "For any role touching systemic payment systems or wholesale settlement, preserving the singleness of money requires timely redeemability into central bank money at par value and elastic provision of liquidity."
解读：把"按面值即时兑换为央行货币 + 弹性流动性供给"明确为可执行的门槛标准，而非抽象原则。
新颖性：中高。重要性：高——决定代币化货币的设计合规边界与央行安全网适用范围。

**观点 40（结论·方法论警示与协作要求）：**
> "Any projections assuming widespread adoption of new instruments and infrastructure remain uncertain given the financial system's flexibility in adjusting to new conditions. … International cooperation will be indispensable to avoid harmful fragmentation or regulatory arbitrage."
解读：对"稳定币将大规模普及"的线性外推作出重要限定（体系会自适应），并强调信息共享、监管学院与统一数据模板等国际合作工具。
新颖性：中。重要性：高。
